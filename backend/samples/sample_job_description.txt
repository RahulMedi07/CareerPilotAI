Senior AI Engineer
Northwind Labs - Remote (US / EU timezones)

About the role
--------------
We are building an AI assistant that reads a company's internal documents and
answers questions about them with citations. You will own the retrieval layer
and the agent orchestration end to end: choosing how documents are chunked and
embedded, deciding when the model should search versus answer directly, and
making the whole thing fast enough to feel instant.

You will work directly with the founding engineers. There is no dedicated ML
platform team, so you should be comfortable owning your work all the way to
production.

What you will do
----------------
- Design and ship retrieval augmented generation (RAG) pipelines over a large,
  messy document corpus.
- Build multi-step agent workflows with LangChain and LangGraph.
- Serve models behind a FastAPI service and keep p95 latency under 800 ms.
- Containerise everything with Docker and deploy to Kubernetes on AWS.
- Evaluate retrieval quality with real metrics, not vibes. Own the eval harness.
- Mentor two junior engineers and review their pull requests.

Requirements
------------
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, at least 2 of them
  building production machine learning or LLM systems.
- Strong Python. You should be comfortable in an async codebase.
- Hands-on experience with FastAPI, LangChain and a vector database such as
  Chroma, Pinecone or pgvector.
- Solid grasp of embeddings, chunking strategies and prompt design.
- Docker and Kubernetes in production.
- PostgreSQL, including how to read a query plan.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.
- Excellent written communication. We are remote and we write things down.

Nice to have
------------
- Experience with Ollama or another local inference runtime.
- Redis for caching.
- Terraform.
- Open source contributions to the LLM tooling ecosystem.
- Experience mentoring or leading a small team.

What we offer
-------------
Competitive salary, meaningful equity, fully remote, and a hardware budget.
We are a 30-person startup that has raised a Series A.
